### Database Strategy

Queralyth will exclusively focus on MySQL during its initial development and product lifecycle.

All core capabilities, including SQL analysis, query processing, execution-plan visualization, performance investigation, and optimization recommendations, will be developed specifically around MySQL.

The primary objective is to develop deep, reliable, and practical database intelligence capabilities for MySQL rather than spreading development resources across multiple database management systems.

Support for other database systems is not part of the current product scope and may be reconsidered as a future expansion opportunity.
